```text

🔒 100 % LOCAL & RESPECTUEUX DE LA VIE PRIVÉE
```

## 2. Store Listing Texts (ENGLISH)
* **Extension Name:** Vision Studio for Google Meet™

* **Short Description:**
    `AR virtual camera for Google Meet™: air drawing with hand tracking, laser pointer, live AR windows and notes locally.`
* **Category :** Productivity / Communication

* **Detailed Description:**


```text
Turn your webcam into an interactive whiteboard and Augmented Reality (AR) studio directly inside Google Meet™—no external software or OBS required.

Vision Studio for Google Meet™ lets you annotate your live video stream using natural hand gestures powered by 100% local computer vision on your device:

✍️ AIR DRAWING, LASER POINTER & SMART SHAPES
• Draw and write in mid-air with your right index finger (smooth Bézier curves and optional Smart Shape recognition).
• Intuitive Dual-Clutch system: close your left fist (or enable Draw Mode and hold Spacebar) to put the pen down in the air.
• Draw Mode is OFF by default so Google Meet™ Push-to-Talk (holding Spacebar) works seamlessly.
• Ephemeral Laser Pointer (strokes fade out after 2 seconds) and finger Eraser.

🖥️ INTERACTIVE AUGMENTED REALITY (LIVE WINDOWS, IMAGES & TEXT CARDS)
• Project up to 3 live application windows (spreadsheets, IDEs, slides), paste clipboard images (Ctrl+V), or spawn floating AR text notes inside your camera feed.
• Automatic gesture detection: pinch with 1 hand to grab and move any AR panel; pinch with 2 hands to zoom and resize.
• Annotations drawn over an AR panel automatically anchor to it and follow its movements.

🎥 SMART DUAL RENDERING (LOCAL MIRROR PREVIEW VS READABLE OUTGOING STREAM)
• You see a natural mirrored preview with your aiming cursor and toolbars (in the resizable studio panel or the 224×126 px mini-preview).
• Meeting participants see an un-mirrored video where your drawings, text notes, and AR windows are placed at the matching position while remaining 100% readable—without ever seeing your cursor or extension UI (unless "HUD Meet" is enabled).
• Instant Passthrough toggle (ON/OFF) to switch back to your native hardware webcam at any time.

🔒 100% LOCAL & PRIVACY-FIRST
• Powered locally by MediaPipe Tasks Vision (WebAssembly/GPU) bundled inside the extension.
• Zero external servers, zero analytics, zero data collection. No video or hand-tracking data ever leaves your browser.

Google Meet is a trademark of Google LLC. This extension is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC.
```
